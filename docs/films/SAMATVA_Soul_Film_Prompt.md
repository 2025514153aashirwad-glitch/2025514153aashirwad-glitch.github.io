## 0. The one idea

**Samatva means “standing level”.** Its two meanings are the whole organisation:

- **Sama, equal:** access for the uncredentialed and the friction-bound, so every individual body of the nation is served.
- **Samatva, even:** a mind that stays level whether a claim passes or fails, and a system that treats feedback *“like a village treats the monsoon: not as an interruption, but as the source of life.”*

The film must leave one feeling: **“I am seen, not steered. What I give rises, and it comes back.”**

## 3. Three directions (brainstorm)

**A · The Medallion (material-led).** One brass medallion in a dark room. The camera moves into the engraving, and each chapter's emblem is struck like a coin in a mint: silver for Reform, bronze for Build, raw cast metal for Generation. It ends on the sun mark. Most premium; heaviest render (Blender).

**B · The Monsoon (metaphor-led).** A thermal world taken from your gradient imagery: orange, red, teal and deep blue on black. Human attention rises as heat (▲), gathers into a lattice like a cloud, and rains back as value (▼). The infinity loop is the water cycle. Most emotional; lightest render.

**C · Seen, Not Steered (people-led).** A citizen, a student, a paraprofessional and a professional, in Bharat, Nepal and Nigeria. Each one's line of sight is drawn as light. The lattice connects their sightlines without touching them, and at the end the sightlines form the mark. Most human; needs real footage or careful illustration.

### Recommended: **A + B braided: “The Vessel and the Light”**
The film climbs the mark from root to sun. Each chapter **opens in the thermal world (B)**, the living, messy reality, and **resolves into metal (A)**, the verified, struck, lasting form. That contrast *is* the Samatwam process: from what you see, to what gets built. People (C) appear as points of light, so there is no stock-footage risk, and they are present in every chapter.

## 5. Look, taken from Aish's own designs

**Three registers, never mixed in the same frame:**
1. **Metal (resolution):** photoreal engraved brass, brushed silver, cobalt enamel, dark warm wood. Lit like your home and Reform heroes: low raking key light and shallow depth of field. *Blender Cycles; HDRI `brown_photostudio_02`.*
2. **Thermal (living reality):** liquid heat gradients (deep blue → violet → orange-red → teal) on black, with posterised bands, slight chromatic fringing and slow flow, as in your “Build and Evolve” card and System hero. The mark appears in pure white inside it.
3. **Editorial (words):** white ground, generous space, black pill elements. Typography:
   - **Geist Medium** headings at −2 % tracking
   - **Inter** body in grey `#A1A1AA` on dark or `#5C5C5C` on light
   - **Archivo**, heavy and uppercase, for manifesto lines on your process blue
   - Devanagari kicker words above English titles (समत्व, सुधार, निर्माण, पीढ़ी) **[CONFIRM Hindi words]**

**Palette:**
- Ink `#131313`

- Action blue `#0066E3` (one use only: the final card's single line, if any)

## 6. Voice, music and language

- **Narrator:** Indian English, calm and warm, unhurried, mid-low. Description for the voice model: *“A calm, warm, slightly deep Indian English voice, unhurried, intimate, very clear audio, no background noise.”*
- **Language versions:** English master, plus Hindi narration and captions **[CONFIRM: also Nepali? Yoruba/Hausa captions for Nigeria?]**.
- **Music:** a tanpura drone on Sa throughout (equanimity). One rhythmic phrase is handed from tabla (Bharat) to madal (Nepal) to talking drum (Nigeria), so the three nations literally pass one idea around the loop. A bell marks each chapter's metal moment. Silence at 0 s and 88 s.
- **Mix:** narration about 10 dB over the bed, −14 LUFS, true peak −1 dBTP.
