# Samatwam 05 · The Threaded Map — Treatment
Gilded Illumination · 1920×1080 · 30 fps · 60 s · English narration, English/Hindi subtitles · −14 LUFS.

## Three candidate structures
1. **Continuous narration on one page** (the brief): one painted page shows the same builder four times; a woven map grows in the border; night falls; a lamp carried across the page lights only the gold of the two held works; the quatrefoil face; the page turns to its plain back.
2. **A year as four pages**: one page per moment. Clearer, but the style is one picture, and the reveal needs everything together.
3. **The map first**: start on the woven grid, then zoom into each cell's moment. Analytic, but it spends the gold too early.

**Chosen: 1.** The style's signature (light only on gold) carries the fact (gold only where work was held).

## Design
- **Gold appears only in two places**: the tablets beside the two held works, and their two cells in the map. The stars in the sky are white-lead dots, not gold, so the rule is visible.
- **Woven map** (the right border panel): time runs down as 12 rows of warp threads (months); disciplines run across as 6 coloured weft threads; stitches by stage (claimed a dot, admitted a dash, submitted a cross, held a gold square). The builder's pattern is a seeded, illustrative example: the brief says every map differs, and it gives no data.
- The builder (vermilion robe) appears at a workbench, at the crossing with a reviewer, teaching a younger learner, and at a field site; a dotted path links the four.

## Beats (60 BPM)
0–5 macro on gold-leaf squares (seams, bole, punch dots), pulling out to the page · 5–20 the four moments · 20–30 the warp and weft grow; stitches appear · 30–36 silence: night, gold laid dark · 36–50 a lamp is carried across: gold flashes square by square at the two held works only; one bell on the first flash · 50–53 push into the quatrefoil face · 53–56 the page turns to its plain vellum back, "the private side" · 56–60 end card in iron-gall ink on the back.

Sound: brush on vellum, leaf crackle, the lamp flame, the page turn; a sarangi line over a slow tabla theka (60 BPM) and a tanpura.
