# Samatwam 04 · Seven Levels of Sight — Treatment
Paper-cut Lightbox · 1920×1080 · 30 fps · 60 s · English narration, English/Hindi subtitles · −14 LUFS.

## Three candidate structures
1. **The lamp at the back, layer by layer** (the brief): a seven-sheet box with the builder by the lamp; each consent burns one window open, and the light reaches a wider world; Sight 6 stays uncut; the strip "consent covers Sight 5"; then Sight 7 opens.
2. **A walk from the front inwards**: the camera enters through the public layer and walks back to the builder. It inverts consent (the world arrives first), so it says the wrong thing.
3. **Seven boxes in a row**: one box per level. Clear, but it loses the single stacked object and the shared light.

**Chosen: 1.** One object and one light; consent is literally how far the light gets.

## Layers (back → front; value ladder bright → dark)
Sight 1 the builder at a bench · Sight 2 mentors · Sight 3 a school · Sight 4 a city · Sight 5 a river and a bridge · Sight 6 hills (the door that stays closed) · Sight 7 the public (a crowd). The labels are "Sight 1"–"Sight 7" only (no level names were supplied). Each sheet carries one arched "consent window": scored (dashed) until consent, then it burns through.

## Beats (66 BPM, beat 0.909 s)
0–5 darkness; the filament ticks, the lamp strikes · 5–18 Sight 1, 2, 3 burn open on the grid · 18–28 the camera pushes into the box: mentors, a school, a city; Sight 4, 5 open · 28–34 silence, framed on Sight 6's uncut window: NOT OPENED · 34–48 pull back; a paper strip writes itself on the front sheet: "consent covers Sight 5"; sheets 6–7 stay dark · 48–56 the builder opens Sight 7: light floods the front sheet, and the crowd appears · 56–60 the lamp on a table in a dark room; the mark glows as a cut-out above it; end card.

Sound: a craft knife scoring before each window, paper rustle as it opens, the lamp filament; a santoor line over a tanpura drone at 66 BPM, each layer on the next note.
