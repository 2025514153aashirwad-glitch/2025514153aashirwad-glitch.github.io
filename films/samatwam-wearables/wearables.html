Every gradient has a cause.

Five gradient styles for five Physical AI wearables, each running its own small model on the device. The simple tell of an AI-made visual is that nothing in it has a cause: light from nowhere, glows on everything, decorative blobs, numbers that mean nothing. Here every gradient is caused by something real: one light on metal, footage colour-mapped, a lens view, fingertip force, machine sound. The Blender plan to replace the drawings with modelled products is in Samatwam Wearables · Blender Plan.

How each clip avoids the tell

One light, placed on purpose

Each studio is lit from one corner; the floor gradient is that light falling off, and highlights on the ring and pendant agree with it.

Gradients carry data

The ring's arc is grip strain, the glove's glow is force per fingertip, the pendant's spectrogram is sound by frequency, the band's footage is mapped through the brand gradient.

Product-page grid

A fixed left column (name, one line, four specs on hairlines), the object on the right, and one saffron chip for the model's decision. Nothing centred for effect.

Honest numbers

Model size, latency and weight are shown with units and marked as targets, not measurements. Every clip is tagged Concept.
