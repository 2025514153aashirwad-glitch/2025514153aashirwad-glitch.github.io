Samatwam · 5 motion-system boards · 6 seconds each · concepts

One grid. Five surfaces.

Each board is a sheet of eight tiles showing one real Samatwam surface in motion: its state ring, its key card, its icons, its inputs or signals, its motion curves, its colour token, one key number and one idle state. Saffron fading to white throughout, with the Samatwam mark as the icon; gold only on the Open Research board, because that is proven work.

Where these are used

Website and pitch decks

Each board explains one product surface in six seconds, the way design studios present a system: as a section header loop on the site, or as the opening slide for that product in a deck.

The design system itself

The tiles are the real tokens: state names, card labels, icon sets, colours and motion curves. Developers building the Samatwam Agent or the Ring app can use them as the spec.

Reels and posts

Any single tile, cropped square, is a 6-second loop for social posts, for example the Agent ring moving from Listening to Answering.

Honest numbers

Figures on the boards (120 ms, 7 days, 4,800, 2 / 3, 14 days) are targets or examples, and each tile says so.
