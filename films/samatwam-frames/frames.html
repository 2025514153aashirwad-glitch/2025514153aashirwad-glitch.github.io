How the prompt book was carried out

Footage first

Every clip runs on real, professionally shot Mixkit video (free for commercial use, no credit required): labs, workshops, classrooms, data centres, Bharat. Each shot is graded and moves slowly, so it reads as filmed rather than assembled.

Liquid glass and metal

Panels blur and saturate the footage behind them, with a bright rim, a specular top and a faint saffron and blue edge where light bends. Controls, seals and step markers are brushed metal; gold is reserved for proven or held work (F09, F17).

Type that moves with intent

Headlines rise letter by letter through masks and leave the same way before the next shot. Numbers count, claims type themselves, and each clip lands on a glass title card for the last 1.5 seconds.

Transitions inside 5 seconds

Most clips carry two shots: a glass slab wipes from one to the next, an iris opens into the second shot, or a metal divider splits the frame. The idea then plays out as a working interface.

Infographics as product UI

Steppers, bar comparisons, rings, live line charts, search bars, honeycombs and checklists, built like screens from a real product rather than slide charts.
