How the prompt book was carried out

Same rules, a lighter engine

The book names Remotion. These clips use the same principle (code only, frame-exact, one command per clip) on the HTML, GSAP and WebGL route already working here, which renders on a cloud computer with no graphics card. The book still runs as written in Claude Code on a computer with a graphics card. Remotion is free for teams of up to 3 people.

Glass, honestly

Panels have a 1 px rim, a specular top edge, an inner shadow and a real background blur of what sits behind them. True lens distortion (drei's transmission material) needs a graphics card, so it is not in these previews.

Silent, 1080p

Sound is left out, as you asked earlier ("loose audio"). These are 1920 × 1080 web cuts. The 4K masters, the vertical 1080 × 1920 recomposes and the 90-second reel wait for your go (phase 7 of the book).

Gold and honesty

Gold appears only in F09 (the proven line and its seal) and F17 (the final seal). Every clip that shows invented figures carries an ILLUSTRATIVE tag. No DPIIT or Startup India mark appears anywhere: their email limits logo use to the approved event.

References as mood only

Your board (liquid UI, glass buttons, octagon portholes, HUD frames, keynote black) set the mood. No layout, photo, logo or text from those references is copied.

Each clip ends on a hold

Frames 0–30 reveal, 30–110 move the idea, 110–150 hold on the title line, with custom easing curves throughout and a three-dot rail that tracks the phase.
