The rules these reels follow

One typeface, no italics

Geist everywhere, the face of your website, in two weights. Emphasis comes from a moving saffron gradient on the key word, never from a second font or a slant.

Safe for the platform

No text in the bottom 20% or the right 12%, where the like, comment and share buttons sit. Captions are burned in for muted viewing.

Every number has a source

Each anchor card names its source on screen. Invented example claims carry an EXAMPLE tag. Gold appears only on proven or held work (R04, R11, R15, R18).

Motion

Real footage graded and slowed, glass wipes and iris cuts, hard cuts on a 120 BPM grid in R06, letter, word and 3D-flip type reveals, scrambled hashes, counters, drawn lines, and a liquid gradient field behind every anchor.
