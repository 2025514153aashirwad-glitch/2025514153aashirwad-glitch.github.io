# Samatwam 01 · The Line of a Work — Treatment

Transit Map style · 1920×1080 · 30 fps · 60 s · English narration, English/Hindi subtitles · −14 LUFS.

The network is a **metaphor** (a work's journey drawn as a line). The cartouche says so: "A diagram of how a work moves through Samatwam". The facts come only from the brief: four stages, six parent themes, the example work, the Crossing, and the sample certificate number. No other numbers, names or dates appear.

## Three candidate structures

1. **One line, stop by stop** (the brief's beats): the Water, Flow & Hydraulics tip draws Claimed → Admitted, the industry line arrives and the interchange falls (The Crossing), the line tunnels under the Review band, surfaces at Held, then levels-of-detail pull back to the six-theme network and the "you are here" veil.
2. **Network first, then rewind**: a low sweep over the finished six-line map, which rewinds to one stop and replays the single journey. Strong as an object shot, but it gives away the ending and the peak (the falling interchange) arrives late.
3. **Two lines compared**: a work that skips review against one that crosses it. Says "proof" well, but invents a counterfactual and steals film 02's subject.

**Chosen: 1.** It keeps the brief's order and timing, puts the native move (the falling interchange) at the emotional peak, and earns the network reveal as scale arriving.

## Logline and arc

One water-filtration work rides its line from Claimed to Held; when the pull-back comes, it is one line on a network of six themes. Setup: the stop lights → Turn: the industry line meets it and the capsule falls; review under the band in near-silence → Ending: Held, the odometer lands on SAM-ENG-2027-0042, the network appears, and one segment stays lit.

## Network (G units; invented, labelled as a diagram)

- Six theme lines, west to east, converge on a north–south **Industry** line (navy; it stands for the partner who posts the need), cross a **Review** water band as tunnels, and fan out after **Held**.
- Stops per line: CLAIMED · ADMITTED · THE CROSSING (interchange with Industry) · SUBMITTED · [tunnel under REVIEW] · HELD. Six lines × five named stops gives 30 named stops and 6 interchanges, plus minor stops.
- The story line: **Water, Flow & Hydraulics**, teal. The accent is red for the "you are here" dot and the Held terminus ring.
- Cartouche (bottom-right margin): "Samatwam · The Line of a Work" / Hindi below; note: "A diagram of how a work moves through Samatwam".
- Counter (top-left, below the series title): STAGE 01 → 02 → 03 → 04, then CERTIFICATE · SAM-ENG-2027-0042 (each held ≥ 2 s).
