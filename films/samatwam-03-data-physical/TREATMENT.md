# Samatwam 03 · What Is Given Back — Treatment
Data Physicalization · 1920×1080 · 24 fps · 60 s · English narration, English/Hindi subtitles · −14 LUFS.

## Three candidate structures
1. **One hundred plates, stack by stack** (the brief): one plate lands → the builders' stack rises to 70 → hold → the commons stack (15) → the remaining stack (15) → pull back to the dial → a brass puck lands at the centre.
2. **All 100 at once, then sorted**: a heap of plates sorts itself into three stacks. Fast, but "growth is assembly" is lost, and plates never move as a heap in this style.
3. **The dial first**: open on the finished dial and read it share by share. Clear, but it spends the signature pull-back at the start.

**Chosen: 1.** It keeps the brief's beats and saves the "chart to installation" pull-back and the last object for the peak.

## Facts and their treatment
1 plate = 1% of what a held work earns (said once, on screen); 70 to the builders (brass, every tenth plate darker as a ruler); 15 to an open commons (tinted acrylic, "open licence by default"); the remaining 15: **its destination is not confirmed, so it is shown as an unlabelled stack** (pale stone, a count only), and narration line 4 is left out until it is confirmed; "Only held works earn" is printed on the dial. Source line: "Samatwam Org, share of value from a held work".
