# SAMATWAM: The Outline and the Record

A 50-second film in the Bauhaus Shapes style. Watch it here: [`samatwam.mp4`](samatwam.mp4)

**The story.** A claim starts as an empty outline (01 Claimed). Each recorded, checkable event fills one square of it, one pixel. The claim travels through the Website, the Studio (02 Admitted) and the Review Desk (03 Submitted, checked from outside, in green). At the Registry the last pixels land, the edge comes out true, and the claim turns gold: 04 Held. The film ends on the wordmark with the four marks: Sun (Prime), Octagon (Creative), Hexagon (Engineering) and Wheel (Forge).

**Colours.** Ink #141414, grey #5A5A5A, line #E2E2E0, green #1F7A3A (only for the outside check), and gold #B8933B (only once the claim is Held).

**Files.** `poster.jpg` is a still from the film, `contact-sheet.jpg` shows one frame every 2 seconds, `TREATMENT.md` is the plan and `CREDITS` lists the font and tools. `index.html`, `mix.py` and `build.sh` are the source, and rebuild the film with the Lemo-Opuscar library.
