# SAMATWAM: The Outline and the Record — Treatment

Style: Bauhaus Shapes. 1920×1080, 30 fps, about 50 s. No voice and no subtitles: the shapes and a few large labels carry it (the style allows a film without a voice). English on screen.

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **A single journey.** One claim travels along a line through the four touchpoints (Website → Studio → Review Desk → Registry). At each one, recorded pixels land inside it until it is complete and turns gold: Held.
2. **Before / after (the ghost).** Two claims of the same size: one with nothing recorded, which stays a grey outline, and one filled pixel by pixel. The outline is the comparison.
3. **A countdown of stages.** The big numerals 01 → 04 build the frame on the grid, each stage adding one kind of shape: Sun, Octagon, Hexagon, Wheel.

**Chosen: 1, with the core of 2 inside it.** The claim is drawn first as an empty compass outline: it shows the size of what is claimed, with nothing proven yet. Recorded pixels fill it, so the film shows Samatwam's rule ("sharpness increases only with recorded pixels") as geometry, which is what this style does well. Structure 3 would show the brand marks but not the idea.

## 2. Logline and arc

*A claim starts as an empty outline; each recorded, checkable event fills one square of it; only when it is completely filled does it become sharp, and gold: Held.*

- **Setup:** a compass draws an empty circle. 01 CLAIMED. One pixel drops in.
- **Turn:** the line through the touchpoints; pixels arrive in batches. The green base at the Review Desk shows the outside check. Then a stop: 97 of 113 filled, with gaps still visible. Silence.
- **Ending:** the last pixels land one at a time, the gaps close, the edge comes out true, and the disc turns gold: 04 HELD. The camera pushes through the gold, and the gold plane shrinks back to a single small gold point beside the wordmark. The film opens on an empty outline and closes on a filled point.

## 3. Benchmark

- **Josef Albers, *Interaction of Color*, and the Vorkurs plates.** Learn: one idea per plate, colour by role, the patience of a held composition. Don't take: any plate's layout.
- **Charles and Ray Eames, *Powers of Ten*.** Learn: one steady move that changes the meaning of scale, and the final push that turns a thing into a field. Don't take: its framing, its narration or its music.

## 6. Cue map: 100 BPM, 4/4, bar = 2.4 s

| Bars | t | Music |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | 0–4.8 | vibraphone, one note per bar; a ruling-pen hiss on each grid line |
| 3–4 | 4.8–9.6 | add a low marimba pulse on beats 1 and 3; one kalimba note for the first pixel |
| 5–13 | 9.6–31.2 | marimba ostinato in eighths, D pentatonic; bass pizzicato on the downbeats; kalimba notes climbing the scale with the count; the chord changes at each station (bar 5, 8, 10) |
| 14 | 31.2–33.6 | whoosh on the whip, then everything stops |
| (gap) | 33.6–34.4 | near-silence: a room tone only |
| 15–17 | 34.4–40.8 | one high kalimba note per last pixel (rising); at Held, a vibraphone chord and a low thud |
| 18–19 | 40.8–45.6 | swelling vibraphone tremolo through the gold; cut on the downbeat |
| 20–21 | 45.6–50.0 | marimba cadence D–A–D, last note left ringing |

Cuts, landings and labels sit on beats: stations at bars 5, 7.5, 10; Held on bar 17, beat 1 (t = 38.4).

## 7. Sound design

| Section | Ambience | Foley | Music |
|---|---|---|---|
| Claim | quiet room | ruling hiss per line, compass scratch, a soft drop for the first pixel | sparse vibraphone |
| Journey | room | small wooden clicks per pixel, a clack as the green base locks, a hiss as the path draws | marimba ostinato with bass |
| Whip / stop | room alone | whoosh | silent |
| Held | room | a click per pixel, then the "seal": a low thud | rising kalimba, vibraphone chord |
| Gold / end | room | a whoosh as the gold opens out, a ruling hiss for the end-card lines | tremolo, then the cadence |

Sound transitions: the marimba enters a beat before the path draws (J-cut into the journey); the Held chord rings over the push into the gold (L-cut).

## 8. Type

Jost (OFL). Stage numeral 800 with tight tracking, its stage name in small caps 700 at +5; the rule line in 300 at +14; touchpoint labels in 600 caps at +4. Text rises out of a slot on its baseline or slides out from under the path. No subtitles.
