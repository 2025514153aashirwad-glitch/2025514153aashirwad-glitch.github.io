# SAMATWAM — Teaser (for schools, industry and partners)

Watch: [`samatwam-teaser.mp4`](samatwam-teaser.mp4) · 62 s · 1080p 60 fps · subtitles in [`samatwam-teaser.srt`](samatwam-teaser.srt) (English with Hindi)

**The idea.** The camera travels up through the Samatwam mark, and each part of the mark is one chapter of a work's journey:

1. **Seed**: a real problem. The six parent themes gather, and one is chosen.
2. **Triangle up**: what enters. Schools bring students, and industry posts a requirement.
3. **Left lobe**: the Studio. Six classes; the work is built and tested by simulation (outlines mean in progress).
4. **The crossing**: review. Claims are tested, one fails and falls away, the work turns solid and is certified, and the Build Record prints.
5. **Right lobe and triangle down**: what is given back. 70% to the builders, 15% to an open Commons.
6. **Spine**: the gates fill as the work rises.
7. **Crescent and emblem**: Engineering, Creative, Forge, Prime. Many bodies, one structure.
8. **Pull back**: the path we flew is the whole mark. SAMATWAM · Build it. Prove it. Give it back.

**Look.** VS-1 theme "Ink, Saffron, Paper"; Space Grotesk, Inter and JetBrains Mono (mono only for records, the sign of proof); shape grammar and fill-as-state from VS-1.

**To check before release.** The mark is a placeholder drawn from the VS-1 anatomy; send the official mark and the film is re-tied to it. The Hindi lines and how the narrator pronounces "Samatwam" need a native check. The requirement and claims are illustrative examples.

`poster.jpg` is the end frame, `contact-sheet.jpg` shows one frame every 2 s, `TREATMENT.md` is the plan, and `CREDITS` lists the fonts and voice. The source (`index.html`, `mix.py`, `vo/`, `build.sh`) rebuilds the film with the Lemo-Opuscar library.
