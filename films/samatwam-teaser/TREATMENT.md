# SAMATWAM — Teaser for schools, industry and partners

1920×1080, 60 fps, about 55 s. English narrator; captions in English with Hindi underneath (the Manifesto pledges both on every Samatwam film). Theme: VS-1 "Ink, Saffron, Paper".

## Source of truth

Samatwam Org Manifesto (Nov 2026) and Visual System VS-1. Only these facts appear: the three verbs, the six classes, the Studio, review and certification ("exactly what was tested"), the 70 % to builders and 15 % to the Commons (pledges, not predictions), and the parent theme names. Cohort predictions (77 works, 50 films) are left out because they are forecasts.

## Three candidate structures

1. **Three verbs as three acts** (Build / Prove / Give back), each a separate set. Clear, but it reads as three slides with a logo at the end.
2. **One work's life story** told through UI: requirement card → Studio screen → certificate. Concrete, but generic SaaS-promo and the mark arrives as an afterthought.
3. **The journey is the mark.** The camera rises through the anatomy of the Samatwam mark: seed → triangle up → left lobe → crossing → right lobe and triangle down → spine → crescent and emblem. Each part is the chapter its VS-1 meaning names. At the end the camera pulls back and the path it flew *is* the mark.

**Chosen: 3.** The founder asked that the storyline be built around the mark. The anatomy already encodes the flow (VS-1 §03, §09: "the left lobe is learning and building, the crossing is review, the right lobe is proof and return"), so the story is Samatwam's own, and the reveal is earned, not stamped on.

## Logline and arc

*A real problem enters as a seed, is built in the Studio, is tested at the crossing, gives back to its builders and the Commons, and rises into the mark: many bodies, one structure.*

Setup: seed and requirement (utility) → Turn: the review at the crossing, outline becomes solid → Ending: give back, rise, pull back to reveal the whole mark.

## Benchmark

- **Apple "Shot on iPhone" / Stripe Sessions title films**: learn the restraint on a light ground (one accent, perfect 1 px lines, type that lands exactly on the beat). Don't take any layout or type.
- **Linear's release films**: learn the continuous camera through interface layers and the mono detail as texture. Don't take their dark palette or glow.

## Visual grammar (VS-1)

- Shapes: circle = person, square = record, triangle up = what enters, triangle down = what is given back, hexagon = Engineering, octagon = Creative, wheel = Forge, sun = the whole.
- Fill as state: outline = in progress, solid = certified.
- Motion verbs: converge (250 ms ease-out), select (200 ms), diverge (400 ms ease-out), loop (steady).
- Lines 1 px (2 px only for the active path). Radius 0 for records, 12 for cards, full for pills. No shadows, glows or gradients; depth comes from surface colour and parallax.
- Type: Space Grotesk (display), Inter (text), JetBrains Mono (records: the sign of proof).
- Colours: surface #FBF7EF, surface-alt #F3ECDD, ink #1C1A17, muted #5E574C, line #E6DFD2, saffron #E07A1F (the active path), gold #B8933B (the mark at the reveal), pass #2E7D32, fail #B3261E; arena accents only on their emblems (Engineering #2F5D8C, Creative #D9662B, Forge #7A5C3E).

## Camera

2.5D world with depth layers (perspective scale by z). One continuous, rising path, with no cuts: push-ins at each station, a lateral track along the lobes, a whip at the crossing, a crane up the spine, and the signature pull-back that reveals the mark.

## Sound

Measured, 96 BPM (bar 2.5 s). Warm synthesized mallet and pluck motif in D, a soft pulse bed, sub hits on every lock (outline → solid), mono ticks under record lines, near-silence before the verdict at the crossing, a swell up the spine, and a resolved chord on the reveal. The narrator sits about 10 dB over the bed; the music dips under each line.

Beat-by-beat timings are set from the narrator's measured line lengths (timeline.json).
