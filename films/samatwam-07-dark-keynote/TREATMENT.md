# Samatwam 07 · Five Layers — Treatment
Dark Tech Keynote · 1920×1080 · 24 fps · 60 s · English narration, English/Hindi subtitles · −14 LUFS.

## Three candidate structures
1. **The still cursor, layers snap around it** (the brief): L1 Individual → L2 Authority → L3 Institutional; accumulation; a freeze; the snap grid with L4 and L5; pull back; one giant line.
2. **Five screens in sequence**: one UI per layer. Clear, but there is no centre, and the "one person" reading is lost.
3. **The public view zooming in to the person**: inverts the order (the world first).

**Chosen: 1.** The accent belongs to the cursor (the person) alone; everything else is cool neutral, and the problem red exists only while the noise exists.
