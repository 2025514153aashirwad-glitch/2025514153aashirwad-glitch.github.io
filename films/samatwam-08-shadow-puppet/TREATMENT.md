# Samatwam 08 · The Creative Arena — Treatment
Shadow Puppetry · 1920×1080 · 24 fps · 60 s · English narration, English/Hindi subtitles · −14 LUFS.

## Three candidate structures
1. **One lamp becomes fifty** (the brief): a lamp lights one figure; behind the screen, hands, rods and a lit tablet (the tools); the creator stages a river, a field, a city; more lamps; the pull toward the lamp; the snap and the creator's own seal; fifty lamps on one long screen.
2. **Fifty first**: open on the long screen and choose one. It loses the growth from one lamp to many.
3. **The tools as the hero**: centre the tablet. It gives Samatwam the vision, which the brief gives to the creator.

**Chosen: 1.** Brightness comes from the number of lamps, as this style requires.

## Facts used
The Creative Arena gathers a cohort; example cohort: 50 creators, 50 five-minute clips; Samatwam provides agents, models, interfaces, tools and creative frameworks (on the tablet); the creator owns the creation (their own carved seal, not Samatwam's); selected works are sponsored into an open series.

## Beats (100 BPM) · dyes: indigo, jade, ochre, flame orange; raw hide; ink
0–5 one oil lamp, a carved figure leans into its glow · 5–11 behind the screen: hands, rods, the tablet · 11–18 the river, the field and the city staged on the cloth · 18–28 six more lamps, more figures · 28–33 silence: the figure is pulled toward the lamp, huge and blurred · 33–44 it snaps sharp; the creator's seal · 44–56 fifty panels on one long screen; "Example cohort · 50 creators · 50 five-minute clips" · 56–60 the mark cut in hide on a lit screen; end card.
Sound: lamp flicker, rods tapping the frame, cloth; dholak, khartal and harmonium, silent for the pull.
